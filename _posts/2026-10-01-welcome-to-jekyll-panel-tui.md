This is a sample post. Replace it with your own: add Markdown files to `_posts/`
named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`.

On the home page, move through posts with **j**/**k** or the arrow keys, open one
with **Enter**, and press **Esc** to come back.

## Code

## Quotes and lists

> Panels, monospace and a blue title bar, like an old BIOS setup screen.

- Categories and tags link to the Tags page, which filters posts.
- Share links and an email line appear below each post.
- Comments use giscus once it's set up in `_config.yml`.
